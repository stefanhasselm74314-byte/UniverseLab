FM-0 dependencies identify O_cosmo, O_growth, O_lensing and O_GW and their

crosswalk is conditional; a cosmology/GW label alone does not activate the gate.
The total remains ten blocking gaps (three partially resolved, seven fully
unresolved). No dormant method gate is added as a new present physical blocker.

QG-PI-01 activates only for its adopted quantum-cosmological branch condition.
QG-GAP-01 activates only for a claimed fundamental geometric minimum used in
singularity avoidance. Their exact canonical requirements are inherited from
the adopted registry. Numerical cutoffs do not establish fundamental gaps.

KK-CTP-01 activates on the use of the specified quantum heavy-mode reduction,
not on its eventual release. A branch cannot evade the gate by using such a
reduction while calling it unreleased. Application of a gate does not itself
mean that the gate has been passed or that physical execution is authorized.

## Regression boundary

The integration validator checks source metadata, inherited requirements,
safe existing paths and JSON pointers, the central and solver entry points,
consumer/gap identity, and status invariants. Negative tests reject dangling
roadmap links, empty evidence lists, dropped gate conditions, unversioned source
URLs, missing provenance, forged closure and physical promotion. The new
workflow runs on every PR and main push to cover dependency-only changes.

Preserved source hashes bind the reviewed baseline. A future change to one of
these dependencies requires an explicit successor/revalidation; it cannot
silently continue to claim this frozen integration baseline. Green QA validates
this documentary contract, not a quantum theory or a physical solver result.
