## 2. Candidate set

### QG-PI-01
**Name:** Gravitational path-integral / saddle-family closure

**Candidate meaning:** A future quantum-cosmological HZT branch is not closed by exhibiting a single regular saddle. It must specify the path-integral contour, gauge/redundancy quotient, relevant saddle family, semiclassical weights and a controlled definition of the saddle sum/path integral.

**Applicability:** activates only if a quantum bounce / no-boundary / Euclidean-complex saddle branch is pursued.

**Not implied:** HZT currently has a necklace divergence; a particular Lorentzian contour is the correct HZT contour.

### QG-GAP-01
**Name:** Invariant geometric-gap derivation

**Candidate meaning:** If HZT claims a fundamental minimum radius, area or volume as part of singularity avoidance, that minimum must be derived from the parent constraint / physical-invariant structure rather than inserted solely as a regulator.

**Applicability:** conditional on HZT actually claiming a fundamental geometric gap.

**Not implied:** every bounce mechanism must contain a geometric gap; the two-vertex LQG result proves an HZT bounce; finite graph anisotropy closes continuum BKL dynamics.

### KK-CTP-01
**Name:** Finite-time in-in heavy-mode closure

**Candidate meaning:** A quantum 6D→4D reduction that traces out KK, radion or bulk modes must construct the finite-time reduced state / influence functional and test nonlocality plus Schwinger-Keldysh branch mixing. A local 4D EFT may be used only after controlled hierarchy and adiabaticity checks.

**Applicability:** quantum reduction of unobserved heavy sectors to cosmological observables.

**Not implied:** current ULSH-07/08 classical/eigenvalue roadmaps are invalid; `m >> H` alone is a universal sufficient condition.
