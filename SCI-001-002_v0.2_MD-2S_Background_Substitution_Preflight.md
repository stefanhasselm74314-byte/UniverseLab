## Metric gate

Define

`A_sigma = sum_s n_s^r A_s'`

`L_sigma = sum_s n_s^r L_s'/L_s`.

Then

`M6^4(-3 A_sigma - L_sigma) = -lambda - Y_sigma/2`,

`M6^4(-4 A_sigma) = -lambda + Y_sigma/2`.

The difference gives

`Y_sigma_required = M6^4(L_sigma - A_sigma)`.

Pure tension requires

`A_sigma = L_sigma`.

## Why lambda_eff is insufficient

A value reconstructed from one junction component fixes only one linear combination of `lambda` and `Y_sigma`. It does not establish equality of the independent `mu nu` and `chi chi` junctions. Therefore the previously reported `lambda_eff` cannot be used to infer a pure-tension PASS.
