Observatory

The Observatory is the interactive background-cosmology and diagnostic layer of UniverseLab. Its calculators and plots are model-evaluation tools; they do not by themselves establish a released 6D forward model or empirical evidence for Hyperzeit.

Open full interactive ObservatoryOpen Validation ConsoleCheck Research Status

What it may test

Background expansion, distances, limiting cases and internally defined parameter responses, subject to the equations and assumptions implemented in the canonical Observatory.

What it may not establish

Ghost freedom, derivation of fitted parameters from the six-dimensional parent action, uniqueness of a dark-sector interpretation, or observational confirmation of HZT.
