GEOMETRY

Origin of the mixed term

K=3H_a+2H_b, K_IJ K^IJ=3H_a²+2H_b², K_IJ K^IJ−K²=−6H_a²−12H_aH_b−2H_b².

The term −12H_aH_b is not an optional interaction coefficient. It follows from squaring the total trace of the extrinsic curvature for a 3+2 spatial product geometry.

RADION

Stationary points and stability

U(b)=A₀b⁻²−k_b b⁻⁴+Q b⁻⁶, U′(b)=−2A₀b⁻³+4k_b b⁻⁵−6Qb⁻⁷. A₀b₀⁴−2k_b b₀²+3Q=0, b_±²=[k_b±√(k_b²−3A₀Q)]/A₀. U″(b₀)=8(3Q−k_b b₀²)/b₀⁸.

A local minimum requires U″(b₀)>0, subject to the model assumptions and canonical normalization of the physical radion.

DEWITT

Negative direction ≠ automatic ghost

The DeWitt supermetric contains an indefinite volume direction. Whether a physical ghost exists must be decided only after constraints, gauge reduction and the quadratic physical action are constructed.

BRST

Constraint chain

Lagrangian → canonical momenta → primary/secondary constraints → first/second class → gauge generator → BRST/physical reduction.

UniverseLab does not treat a formal minisuperspace sign as a completed ghost audit.
