QF-01

Self-adjointness and physical evolution

⟨φ,Hψ⟩=⟨Hφ,ψ⟩, D(H)=D(H†), H=H†, U(t)=exp(−iHt/ℏ), U†U=1.

Formal Hermiticity is insufficient. The operator domain is part of the physical definition. A positive physical norm must be established only after all first-class constraints and gauge identifications have been imposed.

MULTITIME GATE

Integrability of multiple formal times

iℏ∂ₐΨ=HₐΨ ⇒ ∂ₐH_b−∂_bHₐ+(i/ℏ)[Hₐ,H_b]=0.

For time-independent generators this reduces to [Hₐ,H_b]=0. This is necessary, not sufficient: a genuine multitime theory still requires a consistent constraint/gauge reduction and a physical Hilbert space.

RG-01

Renormalization-group audit

k dgᵢ/dk = βᵢ(g), βᵢ(g*)=0, Bᵢⱼ=(∂βᵢ/∂gⱼ)|g*, Bv_I=−θ_Iv_I.

Relevant and irrelevant directions, regulator dependence and truncation stability must be distinguished. Agreement between loop expansions, large-N and functional RG can increase robustness but is not a proof of UV completion.

RED-01

Controlled dimensional reduction

S₆,ε ⟶ S₄,eff with explicit control of compactness, boundary terms, internal gradients and truncation errors.

Γ-convergence and homogenization provide useful mathematical templates, but Lorentzian Einstein gravity is not a coercive elasticity functional. Gauge reduction, constraints and boundary structure must be handled separately.

OBS-01

Observable pipeline

6D parameters → 4D EFT → observables → instrument response → selection → background model → likelihood.

DESI, Pantheon+, KiDS/DES, RSD, SPARC and gravitational-wave data become theory tests only after a released forward map exists. A good fit without that derivation is not evidence for Hyperzeit.

Scientific firewall: numerical stability ≠ ghost freedom; good fit ≠ confirmation; parameter adjustment ≠ derivation from the 6D parent sector; emergent Lorentz symmetry in other systems demonstrates possibility, not identification with HZT.
