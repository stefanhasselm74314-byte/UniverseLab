Mathematical Reference 2.0

English orientation layer for the UniverseLab formula catalogue. Equations, dimensions, assumptions, limiting regimes and status classifications remain governed by the canonical German reference.

ESTABLISHED STRUCTURE

6D gravity

S₆ = ∫ d⁶X √−g [M₆⁴/2 (R−2Λ₆) + L_matter]

The exact matter, boundary and cap sectors must be specified before variation and dimensional reduction.

REFERENCE REGIME

Low-energy decoupling

E, H, k/a ≪ mχ, mKK ⇒ 4D EFT ≃ GR-like regime

This is a regime statement, not observational confirmation of the parent theory.

MODEL DEPENDENT

Radion stability

U′(b*) = 0, U″(b*) > 0, m_b² ∝ U″(b*) / K_b

Positivity requires the correctly normalized physical kinetic coefficient K_b and does not by itself establish stability of the full perturbation system.

GATE DEPENDENT

Observable inference

θ₆D → θ₄D → O_theory → L(data|θ)

The final likelihood stage remains inadmissible wherever the forward map has not been released.

Reference discipline

  • Every equation requires symbol definitions and conventions.
  • Dimensional consistency is checked independently of numerical agreement.
  • IR/UV and weak/strong-coupling regimes must be separated.
  • Constraint reduction precedes claims about physical degrees of freedom or ghost freedom.
