# Band V-B HIGH-Gate Decision v1.0

## Decision semantics

This changeset may close the HIGH public-claim review gate only as a **text/provenance audit result**.

It does not and must not create:

- a physical HZT result;
- a six-dimensional parent derivation;
- an observational likelihood result;
- ghost freedom;
- a solver authorization;
- an `AuthorizationDecision`;
- a `SingleUseGrant`;
- K1-D release;
- K1-E admissibility.

## Required decision condition

`HIGH_GATE_PASS` iff:

- both historical Band-V-A HIGH candidates have a recorded manual contextual adjudication;
- neither is a positive physical HZT claim;
- all required public wording repairs are present;
- re-extraction produces zero current HIGH lexical candidates;
- the append-only current-state successor chain passes the pre-existing strict validator;
- all physical and authorization firewalls remain unchanged.

Otherwise the gate remains fail-closed.
